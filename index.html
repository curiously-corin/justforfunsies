Windowsills
Mold removal + prevention
0/3 ▼
✓
Clean & kill the mold
Concrobium Mold Control + stiff bristle brush
  1. Ventilate: Open a nearby window and put on your N95 mask and rubber gloves.
  2. Spray: Apply Concrobium directly to the mold on the windowsill, coating fully.
  3. Wait: Let it sit for 10–15 minutes.
  4. Scrub: Use the stiff bristle brush to scrub the affected area, focusing on corners.
  5. Wipe clean: Wipe away residue with a damp cloth.
  6. Dry completely: Let the surface dry fully before priming — this is critical.
Concrobium leaves an antimicrobial barrier as it dries — don't rinse it off completely.
✓
Apply Zinsser Mold Blocking Primer
Zinsser spray primer
  1. Confirm surface is dry: The windowsill must be completely dry before priming.
  2. Shake well: Shake the can for 60 seconds before spraying.
  3. Spray evenly: Hold 10–12 inches away and apply a thin, even coat over the cleaned area.
  4. Dry time: Allow 30 minutes to dry. Apply a second coat if needed.
  5. Paint over: Once dry, paint over with your regular wall paint to finish.
Keep the room ventilated — the primer has strong fumes. Keep kids out until fully dry.
✓
Address moisture source
Window insulation kit or dehumidifier
  1. Check weatherstripping: Inspect window seals — replace if cracked or compressed.
  2. Consider a window insulation kit: Plastic film kits reduce condensation significantly in Minnesota winters.
  3. Use a dehumidifier nearby: Keeping humidity below 50% prevents recurrence.
  4. Wipe sills regularly: A quick weekly wipe during winter months catches moisture before it becomes mold.
The mold will come back without fixing the moisture source — this step matters as much as the cleaning.

Shower Grout
Deep mildew removal
0/2 ▼
✓
Apply CLR foaming cleaner & scrub
CLR Mold & Mildew Foaming Stain Remover + grout brush
  1. Ventilate: Turn on the bathroom fan and open a window if possible.
  2. Apply foam: Spray CLR foam directly onto grout lines — the foam clings to vertical surfaces.
  3. Wait: Let sit for 10–15 minutes. Don't let it dry completely.
  4. Scrub: Use the grout brush in firm back-and-forth strokes along each grout line.
  5. Rinse thoroughly: Rinse with warm water until all product is removed.
  6. Repeat if needed: Severe staining may need 2–3 rounds.
A grout brush makes a massive difference — a regular scrub brush won't get into the grout lines effectively.
✓
Restore grout color if still discolored
Grout pen or Polyblend Grout Renew (if needed)
  1. Assess after cleaning: If grout is still deeply discolored after scrubbing, a grout pen is your best option.
  2. Let grout dry fully: Wait 24 hours after cleaning before applying any colorant.
  3. Apply grout pen: Run the pen along each grout line in one smooth pass.
  4. Wipe tile faces: Immediately wipe any product off the tile surface with a damp cloth.
  5. Allow to cure: Let dry fully before using the shower (usually 2–4 hours).
Grout pens work best on white or light gray grout — they essentially restore the original color and reseal at the same time.

Bathtub
Yellow mineral deposit removal
0/1 ▼
✓
Remove yellow mineral deposits
Bar Keepers Friend + non-scratch sponge
  1. Wet the surface: Lightly wet the tub floor.
  2. Apply Bar Keepers Friend: Sprinkle powder (or spray) directly onto the yellow spots.
  3. Let sit: Wait 1–2 minutes — don't let it dry.
  4. Scrub gently: Use a non-scratch sponge in circular motions. Do NOT use abrasive scrubbers — they'll permanently scratch acrylic.
  5. Rinse well: Rinse completely and assess. Repeat for stubborn spots.
  6. Escalate if needed: If spots remain, apply CLR (5 min max on acrylic) then rinse immediately.
Never use steel wool or scotch brite on acrylic — once scratched, it's permanent. Soft cloth or non-scratch sponge only.

Toilets (×5)
Rust rings, under-rim buildup & prevention
0/3 ▼
✓
Deep clean under the rim
CLR Toilet Bowl Cleaner + toilet brush
  1. Apply CLR: Squeeze CLR under the rim, letting it flow down into the bowl.
  2. Coat the bowl: Apply around the waterline and sides of the bowl as well.
  3. Wait 15–20 minutes: Allow CLR to break down mineral and manganese deposits.
  4. Scrub thoroughly: Use the toilet brush to scrub under the rim jets and around the bowl.
  5. Flush to rinse: Flush 1–2 times to clear.
  6. Repeat on all 5 toilets.
The black buildup under the rim is manganese — CLR is specifically effective against it. Let it sit the full time.
✓
Remove waterline rust rings
Pumie Toilet Bowl Ring Remover
  1. Wet the Pumie stick: Dip it in the toilet water to wet it before use.
  2. Scrub the ring: Rub the Pumie along the rust/mineral ring with light, even pressure — it works like an eraser.
  3. Keep both surfaces wet: Rewet the Pumie and ring regularly while scrubbing.
  4. Rinse and assess: Flush and check — repeat as needed for stubborn rings.
  5. Repeat on all 5 toilets.
Pumie is safe on porcelain but don't use it on plastic toilet seats. Light pressure is enough — let the pumice do the work.
✓
Add Iron Out prevention tabs
Iron Out Automatic Toilet Bowl Cleaner
  1. Place one tab in each tank: Drop one Iron Out tab into the toilet tank (not the bowl) of each toilet.
  2. Do not use in bowl directly: The tab goes in the tank so it treats each flush.
  3. Replace as directed: Typically every 30–45 days depending on use frequency.
For rarely used toilets, flush them every few days even if unused — stagnant water is what accelerates the buildup.

Bathroom Sink Drains
Mildew odor elimination
0/1 ▼
✓
Treat drains for biofilm & odor
Drano Max Build Up Remover
  1. Do not use a plunger beforehand: Use on slow or smelly drains — not fully clogged ones.
  2. Pour as directed: Pour the recommended amount slowly down each drain.
  3. Wait overnight: The enzyme formula works best with a long dwell time — ideally 6–8 hours.
  4. Flush with hot water: In the morning, run hot water for 2–3 minutes to flush.
  5. Repeat monthly: Use once a month as maintenance to prevent biofilm from rebuilding.
The smell is from biofilm — hair, soap scum, and bacteria coating the inside of the pipe. Enzymes break it down without harsh chemicals.

Washing Machine
Front loader deep clean
0/2 ▼
✓
Run Affresh cleaning cycle
Affresh Washing Machine Cleaner
  1. Empty the machine: Make sure there's no laundry inside.
  2. Place tablet in drum: Drop one Affresh tablet directly into the washing machine drum (not the dispenser).
  3. Run clean cycle: Select the "Clean Washer" cycle, or the hottest/longest cycle available.
  4. Wipe down after: Once the cycle finishes, wipe the inside of the drum and door glass with a clean cloth.
Run Affresh once a month to prevent buildup. The gasket is the main culprit for front loader smell — wipe it out after every wash if possible.
✓
Clean the door gasket
Damp cloth + mild cleaner
  1. Pull back the rubber gasket: Fold it back to expose the hidden inner lip — this is where mildew hides.
  2. Wipe thoroughly: Use a damp cloth with a little dish soap or diluted white vinegar to wipe all around the inside of the gasket folds.
  3. Dry with a clean cloth: Wipe dry so moisture doesn't sit.
  4. Leave door ajar: After every wash going forward, leave the door cracked open to allow airflow.
Leaving the door open between washes is the single most effective thing you can do to prevent front loader mildew long-term.
